I am currently working as a Research Fellow at Uppsala University, on a project investigating variability in body part semantics and its consequences for body part metaphors, funded by the Royal Swedish Academy of Letters, History and Antiquities (KVHAA).

I obtained my PhD (cum laude) from Radboud University / International Max Planck Research School for Language Sciences in Nijmegen (the Netherlands), working in the *Meaning Culture & Cognition* lab led by Professor Asifa Majid. In my thesis, I investigated semantic variation in colour terms, body part vocabulary, and cutting and breaking descriptions across Japonic, focusing on the endangered Ryukyuan languages.
